RIDER'S COMPASS

How to Choose Your Ideal Bike

Four essential factors that determine which bike geometry and components will serve you best.

🛣️

1. Primary Terrain

Match your tire width and tread to your surface. Smooth tarmac needs 25-32mm slick tires, mixed gravel calls for 38-45mm treads, and technical roots or rocks need 2.2"+ knobby tires.

🧘

2. Riding Posture

Aerodynamic drop bars put you in an aggressive, forward-leaning speed stance. Flat bars offer upright visibility, perfect for navigating traffic and reducing lower back strain.

⚙️

3. Gearing & Elevation

If your daily route has steep hills, prioritize a wide gear range (1x12 or 2x11 setups) or an electric motor. Flat city cruising works wonderfully with simple low-maintenance internal gears.

🎒

4. Cargo & Utility

Planning daily grocery runs or cross-country bikepacking? Look for frames with integrated eyelets for rear pannier racks, full-coverage fenders, and multiple bottle cages.
